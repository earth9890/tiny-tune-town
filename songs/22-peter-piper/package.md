## Description
Welcome to the pepper patch! Ollie the octopus plays Peter Piper and picks peppers with more and more arms: one, two, three, four, five, and then all eight. Bibi the bunny cheers while the crate fills up. Say it fast: pick, pick, pick!

Sing and move along:
- Pick, pick, pick: pretend to pick peppers, a little faster each time
- Count the peppers on your fingers, one, two, three and up
- Say Peter Piper picks, nice and slowly, then fast
- Smile big at the end with Ollie
