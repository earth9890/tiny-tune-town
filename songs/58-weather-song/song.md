# 58: What's the Weather? (original song by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) at a seaside window and garden + Bibi (friend, wears the right hat each pass: sun hat / rain hat / scarf) + crab and turtle (cameos with the kite and snowman). Slot each pass: the weather: sunny / rainy / windy / snowy, then the rainbow.
# Story (visual only): Ollie pulls back the curtain and looks out -> sun shines, Ollie and Bibi play ball -> rain starts, they put on boots and splash in puddles -> wind blows, a kite goes up and Ollie's arms flap like flags -> snow falls and they build a smiling snowman -> the clouds part, sun and rain make a rainbow and everybody cheers.
# Teaching: four kinds of weather (sunny, rainy, windy, snowy) + what to wear or do + the rainbow colours (red, yellow, green, blue). Actions: shine like the sun with your fingers, tap rain on your knees, whoosh with your arms, shiver, clap.
# Rhymes: see/be (every pass); hooray/play, pat/hat, whoo/blue, slow/snow, away/play (one per pass); three/me (every pass); finale snow/go, too/blue.
caption: cheerful children's nursery sing-along, warm clear young female kids-show lead singer, very clear diction, little vibrato, small happy kids choir on the last chorus, quiet soft backing of marimba, ukulele, soft hand claps and light acoustic bass, simple repetitive sing-along melody, short instrumental breaks between verses, major key, happy, sunny, playful, vocals loud and clear on top of the music
type: routine
