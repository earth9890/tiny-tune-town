## Description
Jack and Jill climb the hill to fetch a pail of water, and Ollie and Bibi follow along. Oops, Jack bumps his crown and Jill tumbles after! A bandage, a big hug and some careful steps later, everybody walks up the hill again, fills the pails and heads home under a rainbow.

Sing and move along:
- Pat your head gently when Jack bumps his crown
- Count the bandage: one, two, three
- Give yourself a big hug
- March slowly on the spot: step by step, nice and slow
- Splash, splash like water in the pail
