## Description
Tap, tap, tap! The old turtle taps a happy beat with Ollie the octopus and his friends. Count along with them: one on the sun, two on a shoe, three on the knee, four on the door, five with a big splash dive, and ten once again. Then the puppy gets a bone and everybody rolls home together.

Sing and move along:
- Tap with one finger on your hand
- Tap your shoe
- Tap your knee
- Knock, knock on a pretend door
- Dive with a splash
- Roll your hands, roll, roll, roll along
- Pat, pat, pat the puppy on his back
