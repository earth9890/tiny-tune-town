## Description
Do you know the Muffin Man who lives on Drury Lane? Ollie the octopus and his best friend Bibi the bunny visit the kind baker's bakery. Every time we sing, a new treat pops out of the oven: muffins, buns, apple pie and fresh warm bread, and Ollie catches them all with his eight arms! Then everyone says thank you at a yummy treat party.

Sing and move along:
- Sniff the yummy smell: Ooh!
- Name each treat as it pops out of the oven
- Pretend to juggle like Ollie with all your arms
- Rub your tummy: yum, yum, yum!
- Wave and say "Thank you, Muffin Man!"
