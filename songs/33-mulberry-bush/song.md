# 33: Here We Go Round the Mulberry Bush (traditional singing game, public domain (Halliwell, Popular Rhymes and Nursery Tales, London, 1849; The Real Mother Goose, Rand McNally, 1916)) — new verses and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) leads a ring dance round a berry bush on a sunny beach, with Bibi, a crab and a fish. Slot each pass: the action we do: clap / stamp / pat / spin / jump / bow.
# Story (visual only): friends hold hands round a leafy bush -> clap hands on the sand -> stamp feet -> pat knees in the breeze -> spin round -> jump up high -> take a bow -> everyone waves goodbye to the sea and the sky.
# Teaching: body actions (hands, feet, knees), moving in a circle together. Actions: clap, stamp, pat, spin, jump, bow, wave.
# Rhymes: hands/sands, feet/beat, knees/breeze, around/ground, high/sky, bow/now (slot couplet each pass); bush/push (every pass); sky/goodbye, three/me.
