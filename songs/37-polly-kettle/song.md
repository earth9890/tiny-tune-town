# 37: Polly Put the Kettle On (traditional, public domain (Halliwell, The Nursery Rhymes of England, London, 1842, no. 131; also The Real Mother Goose, Rand McNally, 1916); "Sukey" becomes Ollie) — new verses and music by Tiny Tune Town
# Cast: Ollie (hero) hosts a tea party at a seaside table with Polly (a kind little turtle who puts the kettle on, drawn in this song's scenes.js) + guests Bibi, a crab, a fish and a star. Slot each pass: who comes for tea: Bibi / crab / fish / star / everyone.
# Story (visual only): Polly the turtle puts a big kettle on the stove, Ollie sets out cups with 8 arms -> Bibi arrives with a flower -> a crab clicks in -> a fish swims to the table -> a little star twinkles in -> everyone is at the table, Ollie takes the kettle off and pours (warm, never hot), everyone blows and sips.
# Teaching: manners (welcome guests, say thank you), counting cups. Actions: pour, blow on your tea, sip, say thank you.
# Rhymes: tea/me (every pass), again/ten, hot/lot, three/tea.
