## Description
Sing along with the 12 Names of Ganesha! A gentle, happy song for little ones, with the Sanskrit name, the transliteration and a short English meaning on screen for every name, and the joyful refrain "Ganpati Bappa Morya!" (Hail Ganpati!).

The 12 names come from the traditional Sankatanashana Ganesha Stotra (from the Narada Purana):

1. वक्रतुण्ड - Vakratunda - curly trunk
2. एकदन्त - Ekadanta - one big tusk
3. कृष्णपिङ्गाक्ष - Krishnapingaksha - kind brown eyes
4. गजवक्त्र - Gajavaktra - elephant face
5. लम्बोदर - Lambodara - round tummy
6. विकट - Vikata - mighty and big
7. विघ्नराज - Vighnaraja - clears the way
8. धूम्रवर्ण - Dhumravarna - smoky gray
9. भालचन्द्र - Bhalachandra - moon on brow
10. विनायक - Vinayaka - wise leader
11. गणपति - Ganapati - leader of friends
12. गजानन - Gajanana - elephant face

Refrain: गणपति बप्पा मोरया - Ganpati Bappa Morya!

Note for parents: the meanings are short, simple summaries for children, not full translations. Sources differ slightly in spelling and wording of the names. We have tried to keep this respectful and correct; please tell us kindly if you spot a pronunciation mistake.

The second round is for singing along: sing with the kids, name by name!
