## Description
The bells go ding, dang, dong and it is time to wake up! Is Ollie still in bed? Is Bibi? Is the fish in its bowl, or the sun behind the hill? One by one the sleepyheads wake up and then everyone sings good morning together.

Sing and move along:
- Swing your arms like a bell: ding, dang, dong
- Stretch and yawn like a sleepyhead
- Peek-a-boo from under the quilt
- Wave good morning to the sun
- Clap hooray!
