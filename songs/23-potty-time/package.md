## Description
Ollie the octopus tells his grown-up "I need to go!" and takes it step by step: pants down with a helping hand, sit, wipe, flush, wash and dry his hands, and then a big happy dance. Bibi cheers from the doorway and a gold star goes on the chart. Potty time, hooray!

Sing and move along:
- Say "I need to go!" and tell your grown-up
- Count one, two, three with Ollie
- Tap, tap, tap your feet
- Wave bye-bye to the flush and twirl
- Clap, clap, spin around, stomp, stomp on the ground
