# 34: Pop Goes the Weasel (traditional English dance tune and rhyme, public domain (published in London as an "old English dance" in 1853; verses sung on the London stage by 1856); the City Road lines are NOT used — new gentle verses and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) winds up jack-in-the-boxes in a toy room with Bibi; each box holds a springy toy weasel (a fluffy cream toy with a long body, drawn in this song's scenes.js). Slot each pass: the box colour: red / blue / green / yellow / pink / rainbow.
# Story (visual only): Ollie finds a red box and turns the handle -> the music tinkles -> POP! a toy weasel springs out, Ollie jumps and giggles -> a blue box, Bibi turns it -> green -> yellow -> pink (Bibi pops up out of it) -> a rainbow box: all the toy weasels pop out together, everyone laughs.
# Teaching: colours (red, blue, green, yellow, pink, rainbow); anticipation, wait for the POP. Actions: turn a pretend handle round and round, crouch down, jump up on "Pop!".
# Rhymes: round/sound (every pass), goes/weasel (the classic's own near rhyme, every pass); chorus: me/three.
