# 25: Colours Song (original song: lyrics & music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero, a pink octopus who can change colour) in the sea with a new friend each pass: red crab, blue whale, yellow sun, green turtle, purple shell. Slot each pass: the colour + its sea or beach friend.
# Story (visual only): pink Ollie meets a red crab -> pop, Ollie turns red too (octopus magic!) -> the blue whale sings and Ollie goes blue -> the sun comes out, Ollie goes yellow -> the green turtle swims slowly, Ollie goes green -> a purple shell on the sand, Ollie goes purple -> every friend gathers round and Ollie pops back to his own pink.
# Teaching: the colours red, blue, yellow, green, purple, pink. Actions: snap like a crab, sing "whoo" like a whale, reach up to the sun, walk slow like a turtle, hold a shell in your hand.
caption: cheerful bouncy children's nursery sing-along, warm clear young female kids-show lead singer, very clear diction, little vibrato, small happy kids choir on the last chorus, ukulele, glockenspiel, hand claps, light bass, small drums, simple repetitive sing-along melody, short instrumental breaks between verses, major key, happy, sunny, playful, clean bright mix
