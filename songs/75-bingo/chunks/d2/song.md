# 75: Bingo (traditional, public domain: English-language folk song "There was a farmer had a dog", published 1780 (Wikipedia "Bingo (folk song)", earliest transcript in the 1785 songbook The Humming Bird); first verse is traditional, the later verses only swap letters for claps, as in every folk version; music is new)
# Cast: a friendly farmer (Ollie in a straw hat on a hill farm), Bingo the puppy, Bibi. Slot each pass: letters replaced by claps (none, B, B-I, B-I-N, B-I-N-G, all).
# Story (visual only): Bingo the puppy sits by the farm gate; each verse a letter of the sign B-I-N-G-O turns into a clapping hand picture; at the end Bingo gets a bone and a big hug. Teaching: spelling B-I-N-G-O, rhythm clapping.
# Rhymes: dog/name-oh (classic), oh/O.
