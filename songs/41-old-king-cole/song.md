# 41: Old King Cole (traditional, public domain: first attested in William King's "Useful Transactions in Philosophy", London, 1709; the modern text is in The Real Mother Goose, Rand McNally, 1916 — the pipe and bowl are left out; new verses and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) as the merry King Cole in a golden crown on a big cushion throne + Bibi (fiddle) and three music friends (a crab with a drum, a fish with a flute, a star with a bell), drawn in this song's scenes.js. Slot each pass: the instrument: fiddle / drum / flute / bell.
# Story (visual only): King Ollie yawns on his throne and says "Music, please!" -> Bibi saws a happy fiddle and the notes float up as little hearts -> the crab drums, the crown bounces -> the fish toots a flute, bubbles come out the end -> the star rings a bell and everyone dances -> all four play together and the King conducts with eight arms.
# Teaching: musical instruments and their sounds (tweedle, rum-tum, toot, ding). Actions: play a pretend fiddle, drum, flute and bell.
# Rhymes: Cole/soul, he/three, dee/me (every pass); finale 3 more of the same.
