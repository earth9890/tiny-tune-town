# 47: Ring a Ring o' Roses (traditional, public domain: first printed in Ann S. Stephens's novel The Old Homestead, 1855, and in Kate Greenaway's Mother Goose, London, 1881; text as in The Real Mother Goose, Rand McNally, 1916 — the "fall down" ending is changed to a giggle and a sit-down; new verses and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) + Bibi + the sea friends (fish, crab, star, turtle) holding hands in a ring on a flower meadow (a rose bush and posy basket drawn in this song's scenes.js). Slot each pass: how we go round: walk / hop / tiptoe / spin.
# Story (visual only): Ollie picks a posy of roses -> Bibi takes his hand, then the fish, crab, star and turtle join the ring -> everyone circles slowly (the camera circles too) -> hopping, tiptoeing, spinning; on "ah choo" Ollie sneezes a cloud of petals -> everyone sits down softly on the grass laughing -> a final ring with the posy in the middle.
# Teaching: going round in a circle, a sneeze and "bless you", sitting down gently. Actions: hold hands and circle, hop, tiptoe, spin, a pretend sneeze and a giggle.
