## Description
Five little ducks swim in the sun, splash, splash, splash! One little duck hides, then another, and Ollie the octopus counts them down from five to one. At the end Mama Duck calls quack, quack, quack and all five ducklings hurry back. A gentle counting sing-along for toddlers and preschoolers, with a new tune and new words.

Sing and move along:
- Hold up five fingers and put one down each time a duck hides
- Flap your arms like little wings and say quack, quack, quack
- Cover your eyes for hide and seek
- Open your arms wide when Mama calls the ducklings back
