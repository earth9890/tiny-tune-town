## Description
Time to go out! Ollie the octopus buckles two more shoes every verse: one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight. Knock knock, Bibi the bunny is at the door, and a fat hen joins in to make ten. With eight shoes on, off they go to play!

Sing and move along:
- Count with your fingers, one, two, three and up to ten
- Click, click, click: pretend to buckle your shoe
- Knock, knock at the door
- Lay your sticks straight, then clap for the hen
- March out the door: hooray, hooray!
