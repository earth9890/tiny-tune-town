# 30: This Old Man (traditional, public domain (Welsh/English folk rhyme; a version printed in English Folk-Songs for Schools, Baring-Gould & Sharp, London, 1906; collected by A. G. Gilchrist from a Welsh nurse in the 1870s) — new verses and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) drums on things with a friendly old turtle ("this old man") + Bibi; a puppy joins the dog verses. Slot each pass: the number and the thing he taps: thumb-free version: sun / shoe / knee / door / dive / again.
# Story (visual only): the old turtle taps a beat with Ollie on the sand -> a shoe -> Ollie's knee -> a shell door -> a splashy dive with Bibi -> a final tap again -> everyone rolls home together (the puppy gets its bone).
# Teaching: counting 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 (then ten). Actions: tap with one finger, tap your shoe, tap your knee, knock on a door, pretend to dive, roll your hands.
# Rhymes: one/sun, two/shoe, three/knee, four/door, five/dive, ten/again, whack/back, bone/home (classic's own near rhyme), along/song.
