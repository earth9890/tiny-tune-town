# 40: The Grand Old Duke of York (traditional, public domain: an "Old Tarlton's song" version in Tarlton's Jests, ed. Halliwell, 1844; modern wording first printed in Arthur Rackham's Mother Goose, London, 1913 — new verses and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) as the grand old Duke in a paper crown, leading ten little friends (Bibi, fish, crab, star, turtle and friends, drawn in this song's scenes.js) over a green hill. Slot each pass: how they go: marched / hopped / tiptoed / stomped.
# Story (visual only): Ollie puts on a paper crown at the bottom of a sunny hill -> the friends line up behind him -> they march up, up to the top and wave -> they march down again -> hopping, then tiptoeing (finger on lips, a sleeping snail), then stomping with big puffs of dust -> all ten reach the top and cheer; Ollie bows and the crown wobbles.
# Teaching: up and down, counting ten friends. Actions: arms up high, arms down low, march, hop, tiptoe, stomp.
# Rhymes: then/again, high/sky, low/toe; finale hill/still, hooray/day.
