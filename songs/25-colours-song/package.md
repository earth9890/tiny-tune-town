## Description
Ollie the octopus meets a new friend on the beach each verse, a red crab, a blue whale, a yellow sun, a green turtle and a purple shell. Every time, Ollie asks "What colour can he be?" and then turns that colour himself! Shout the answers and snap, sing and hop along.

Sing and move along:
- Snap like a crab for red
- Sing whoo like a whale for blue
- Reach up high to the sun for yellow
- Walk slow like a turtle for green
- Hold a shell in your hand for purple
- Shout the colour when Ollie asks
