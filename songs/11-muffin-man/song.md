# 11: The Muffin Man (traditional, public domain (c. 1820, manuscript in the Bodleian Library, Oxford) — new verses and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) + Bibi (friend) + Baker Bun (the kind round baker, new in this song). Slot each pass: the treat: muffin, bun, pie, bread.
# Story (visual only): Ollie and Bibi ring the bell of the bakery on Drury Lane -> the baker waves them in, a muffin pops out of the oven -> each pass a new treat pops out and lands in one more of Ollie's arms -> by the bread Ollie juggles 8 treats, wobble wobble, Bibi catches the bread on a tray -> treat party at the big table, everyone says thank you.
# Teaching: names of baked foods (muffin, bun, pie, bread) + counting treats in Ollie's arms. Actions: knock knock, clap, pretend juggling, yum yum yum.
