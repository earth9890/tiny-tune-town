## Description
Sing along with Farmer Mac and his farm friends! A cow, a pig, a duck and a sheep join the farm one by one, and every friend has a sound for you to sing.

Do it with us:
- Stomp your feet with the cow: moo moo!
- Wiggle your nose with the pig: oink oink!
- Flap your wings with the duck: quack quack!
- Jump up high with the sheep: baa baa!
- Then clap your hands and shout hooray!
