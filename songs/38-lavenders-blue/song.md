# 38: Lavender's Blue (traditional English love song and lullaby, public domain (broadside c. 1672; text as in The Only True Mother Goose Melodies, Munroe & Francis, Boston, 1833, and Halliwell, 1842); the original's "call up your men" verses are NOT used) — new gentle bedtime verses and music by Tiny Tune Town
# Cast: Ollie (hero) tucks in for the night in a cosy lavender seaside cave with Bibi; moon, stars and soft waves outside. Slot each pass: what shines or sleeps: moon / stars / waves / Bibi, ending on the classic verse.
# Story (visual only): lavender fields glow blue and green at dusk, Ollie and Bibi walk home -> the moon rises bright -> the stars come out one by one -> the waves go white and slow -> Bibi yawns and cuddles a pillow -> Ollie pulls up a blanket, the whole sea goes to sleep, a soft last glow.
# Teaching: bedtime wind-down (moon, stars, close your eyes). Actions: slow yawn, hug your pillow, close your eyes, whisper night-night.
# Rhymes: green/queen (classic, passes 1 and last), bright/night, light/night, white/night, tight/night.
caption: bright high-pitched sweet young female children's TV singer, soft clear lead vocals, very clear diction, little vibrato, small gentle kids choir on the last verse, quiet soft backing of music box, celesta and soft strings, gentle bedtime lullaby, simple repetitive sing-along melody, slow and dreamy, no drums, major key, sweet, cozy, dreamy, vocals loud and clear on top of the music
