# 21: Jack and Jill (traditional, public domain (first printed in Mother Goose's Melody, London, c. 1765; text as in The Real Mother Goose, Rand McNally, 1916, and Mother Goose or the Old Nursery Rhymes, Kate Greenaway, 1881) — gentler new verses and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) + Bibi, the friends who walk up the hill with Jack and Jill (a boy and a girl drawn in this song's scenes.js) and carry the pails. Slot each pass: the helping step: classic fall / bandage / hug and hold hands / dip the pail.
# Story (visual only): sunny hill with a well on top; Ollie and Bibi follow Jack and Jill up the path, each with a pail -> Jack slips on a pebble, bumps his crown, Jill tumbles after (soft, grass, no pain) -> Ollie dabs a bandage on Jack's head with one arm -> Ollie's 8 arms hug everyone, then all hold hands and walk up again with careful feet -> the pails fill at the well, down the hill with the pails full, a rainbow.
# Teaching: kindness after a bump (bandage, hug, try again); counting to 3; careful steps. Actions: pat your head, hug yourself, walk slow on the spot, splash.
# Rhymes: hill/Jill, three/see, Jill/hill, goes/flows; the tag slow/go (every pass); hooray/day. The classic's own near rhyme: water/after.
# Safety: the classic's "broke his crown" is softened to "bumped his crown", the fix is a bandage and a hug, then they try again carefully (no vinegar and brown paper).
