## Description
Shh, it's bedtime! Big brother Kofi plays Wee Willie Winkie in his nightgown with a little lantern. He and Nomi tiptoe through the house at eight o'clock to see who is asleep: Mango the cat in the basket, Nana in her armchair, and Nomi in her cosy bed. Then Mum and Dad tuck Kofi in. A gentle bedtime lullaby of the classic nursery rhyme for toddlers and preschoolers.

Sing and move along:
- Tiptoe quietly: shh!
- Tap, tap at the window
- Count to eight o'clock on the clock
- Yawn and stretch
- Close your eyes and snuggle up in bed
