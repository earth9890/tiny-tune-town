# 24: London Bridge (traditional, public domain (rhyme first printed in the 1700s; text checked in The Real Mother Goose, Rand McNally, 1916, Project Gutenberg #10607: "London Bridge is broken down ... Build it up with wood and clay") — gentle new verses and music by Tiny Tune Town; the "silver and gold stolen / prison" verses are left out)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) + Crab, Fish, Turtle (sea side) + Bibi (beach side), all from the shared cast. Slot each pass: what they build the bridge with: sticks / bricks / shells / sand.
# Story (visual only): an old plank bridge between the reef and the beach has broken down into the water -> Ollie frowns, friends come to help -> sticks make the posts (a wobble, then a steady stack) -> red bricks make the arches (Crab stacks, Turtle carries) -> shells line the railing (Fish brings them) -> sand makes the soft ramp up to the beach (Bibi pats it down) -> the bridge is finished and everyone crosses from the sea to the sand with Ollie leading, hearts and sunshine.
# Teaching: building and materials (sticks, bricks, shells, sand), counting one, two, three, sea and land. Actions: stack with both hands, clap clap clap, hop over the bridge.
# Rhymes: down/frown, see/three (opening); row/go (every pass); hooray/today, done/fun, row/go (finale). Verified: row/go, done/fun, hooray/today are perfect rhymes.
