# 68: Hip Hip Hooray Party (original song: lyrics & music by Tiny Tune Town; our own birthday song, not the traditional one)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) + Bibi (the birthday friend, wearing a crown) + fish, crab, turtle at a reef-side party. Slot each pass: balloons / hats / candles / presents, then the dance.

# Teaching: party words (balloon, hat, candle, present). Actions: clap, wiggle your arms and toes, hug.
