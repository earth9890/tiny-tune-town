## Description
Row, row, row your boat with Pip! Sail down a sunny river and meet new friends: a duck says quack, a frog says ribbit, a fish says blub, and one by one they all hop in the boat until four friends row home together. Hooray!

Sing and move along:
- Row your arms forward and back, just like Pip, every time we sing "row, row, row".
- Quack with your hands, hop like a frog, and make fishy lips for "blub, blub".
- Count the friends in the boat: 1, 2, 3, 4!
- Wave your arms up high and shout HOORAY at the end.
