## Description
There was a farmer who had a dog, and Bingo was his name-o! Farmer Ollie the octopus and Bingo the puppy sing the classic B-I-N-G-O song with a brand new tune. Each verse one more letter turns into a clap. A fun spelling and rhythm sing-along for toddlers, preschoolers and kindergarten kids.

Sing and move along:
- Spell B-I-N-G-O out loud and point to each letter
- Clap on the letters that disappear
- Wag your hand like a puppy tail
- Give Bingo a big hug at the end
