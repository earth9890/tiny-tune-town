# 52: Hickety Pickety Hen (traditional, public domain (Halliwell, The Nursery Rhymes of England, 1842, Project Gutenberg #32415; also The Real Mother Goose, Rand McNally, 1916, Project Gutenberg #10607) — kid-friendly rewrite of the last word, new verses and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) at a sunny farm henhouse + Bibi (friend, collects eggs in a basket) + turtle (slow visitor, arrives last) + four hens, each in the pass colour, drawn in this song's scenes.js. Slot each pass: the hen's colour: black / white / brown / red.
# Story (visual only): morning at the coop, Ollie and Bibi wait with a basket -> the black hen cluck-clucks and lays one egg, Ollie cheers -> the white hen lays two eggs, Bibi carries them -> the brown hen lays three, the turtle arrives slowly -> the red hen lays four and the basket is full -> a happy egg breakfast table where all eight arms hold a spoon.
# Teaching: colours (black, white, brown, red) + counting eggs 1-2-3 + hen sound (cluck). Actions: flap your wing-arms, cluck, bob your head, clap.
# Rhymes: hen/again, day/lay (every pass); three/me (every pass); finale hooray/today.
