## Description
Meet Ollie, the little octopus who loves to wiggle! Count Ollie's arms from one to eight, then shout the answer: "Who can wiggle? OLLIE-O!"

Sing and move along:
- wiggle one arm, then two, as Ollie counts;
- clap, clap, clap with Ollie and a little crab;
- spin around and pop the bubbles;
- reach up high and wiggle down low;
- open your arms wide and give a great big octopus hug!
