# 22: Peter Piper Picks Peppers (traditional tongue twister, public domain: first printed 1813 in John Harris's "Peter Piper's Practical Principles of Plain and Perfect Pronunciation", London; also in Halliwell's "The Nursery Rhymes of England" 1842 and "The Real Mother Goose" 1916. New verses, tag line and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie plays Peter Piper, the pepper picker, in a sunny pepper patch + Bibi (friend, holds the basket). Slot each pass: the count, one / two / three / four / five / eight. Every pass the "pick, pick" run gets one pick longer, so it feels faster while the tempo stays put.
# Story (visual only): the patch is full of red, green, yellow and orange peppers, Ollie picks one with one arm, then two, three, four, five... plop into Bibi's basket -> the basket fills -> all eight arms pick at once, whoosh -> a big pile and everyone smiles. Teaching: counting 1-5 then 8, colours of peppers. Actions: pick (reach up), plop, tongue-twister clap.
# Rhymes: pick/quick (every pass), too/you, pile/smile
caption: cheerful children's tongue twister sing-along, warm clear young female kids-show lead singer, very clear diction, little vibrato, small happy kids choir on the last chorus, ukulele, hand claps, glockenspiel, light acoustic bass, bouncy steady beat, simple repetitive sing-along melody, short instrumental breaks between verses, major key, happy, sunny, playful, clean bright mix
