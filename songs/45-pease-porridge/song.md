# Cast: Ollie (hero) + Bibi, sitting face to face at a kitchen table with a big steaming pot, a bowl each (a pot and spoons drawn in this song's scenes.js). Slot each pass: the action: clap / pat / stomp / tap.
# Story (visual only): Ollie stirs a pot, steam curls up -> Bibi sits opposite, they clap hands together on "hot" and "cold" -> pat their knees, a little pink bowl bounces -> stomp feet, the spoon dances -> tap the table, steam makes a smiley face -> all eight arms clap with Bibi, then the two share a warm bowl and a giggle.
# Teaching: hot and cold, rhythm and clapping together, taking turns. Actions: clap, pat knees, stomp, tap (with a partner if you have one).
