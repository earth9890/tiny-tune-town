# 36: Jack Be Nimble (traditional, public domain (Halliwell, The Nursery Rhymes of England, London, 1842; also The Real Mother Goose, Rand McNally, 1916); "Jack" becomes Ollie and the candlestick becomes a stick on the sand, so there is no fire to copy) — new verses and music by Tiny Tune Town
# Cast: Ollie (hero) jumps over sticks on a sunny beach with Bibi and a crab cheering. Slot each pass: the kind of stick: tiny / long / big / tall / wiggly / sparkly.
# Story (visual only): Ollie lines up a tiny stick on the sand and hops over, all 8 arms up -> a long stick -> a big one (Bibi counts) -> a tall stick (a huge spring) -> a wiggly stick that wriggles and giggles -> a sparkly stick with a trail of glitter -> everybody jumps together and lands in a soft hug.
# Teaching: size words (tiny, long, big, tall); jumping safely and landing softly. Actions: crouch, jump on "jump over", land with a "ta-da".
# Rhymes: quick/stick (every pass); chorus: three/me, hill/still, land/hand, kick/quick.
