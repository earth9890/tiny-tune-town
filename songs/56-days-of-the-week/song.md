# 56: Seven Happy Days (original song by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) in a cosy home and garden, with a big wall calendar + Bibi (friend, joins in from Wednesday) + crab and turtle (cameo guests on Friday and Saturday). Slot each pass: the day and what Ollie does: Monday play / Tuesday bake / Wednesday run / Thursday sing / Friday hop / Saturday ride / Sunday rest.
# Story (visual only): the calendar page flips to each day and Ollie does the day's activity: stacks blocks -> ices a cake with Bibi -> runs on the grass in the sun -> sings with a tiny microphone while the fish dances -> hops across stepping stones -> rides a little bike with Bibi beside him -> naps in a hug under a blanket on Sunday, the calendar page turns back to Monday.
# Teaching: the seven days of the week in order + simple activities. Actions: stack blocks, stir the cake, run on the spot, sing, hop, pedal, then a big hug on Sunday.
# Rhymes: play/day, bake/cake, run/sun, sing/ding, hop/stop, ride/side, rest/best (one per day); hooray/day (every pass); finale too/through.
