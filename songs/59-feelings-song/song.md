# 59: Feelings and Hugs (original song by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) in a cosy playroom + Bibi (friend, the first hugger), turtle (hugs when Ollie is scared), crab (sits beside him while he breathes), fish (cameo, yawns at the end). Slot each pass: the feeling: happy / sad / mad / scared / sleepy, and the kind thing that helps.
# Story (visual only): Ollie bounces and dances when he is happy -> a toy rolls away, Ollie's lip wobbles and Bibi gives a big hug -> a tower falls, Ollie's face goes red, crab sits beside him and they breathe in and out together until he is calm -> a shadow on the wall, Ollie hides and turtle hugs him, the shadow is just a hat -> sleepy yawns and everyone snuggles under one blanket.
# Teaching: naming five feelings (happy, sad, mad, scared, sleepy) and a kind way to handle each (play, hug, breathe, friends, snuggle). Actions: dance, rub your eyes, deep breath in and out, hug yourself, yawn.
# Rhymes: hooray/play, hoo/through, red/head, hide/side, yawn/dawn (one per pass); three/me, go/so (every pass); finale scared/shared, okay/day.
