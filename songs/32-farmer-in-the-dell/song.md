# 32: The Farmer in the Dell (traditional singing game, public domain (German folk origin; English version printed in W. W. Newell, Games and Songs of American Children, Harper & Brothers, New York, 1883) — new verses and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) joins a friendly farmer's ring game in a sunny dell with Bibi; the farmer, wife, dog, cat and mouse are drawn in this song's scenes.js. Slot each pass: who joins the ring: farmer / wife / dog / cat / mouse / cheese.
# Story (visual only): the farmer rings a little bell and Ollie skips over -> the farmer takes his wife's hand -> a dog jumps onto a log -> a cat in a hat -> a mouse and the cat skip round the house -> the mouse shares the cheese and sneezes -> everyone joins hands in one big ring.
# Teaching: who joins next (a chain of friends), animal sounds (woof, meow, squeak), holding hands in a circle. Actions: skip in a ring, ring a pretend bell, wave hello.
# Rhymes: dell/bell, wife/life, dog/log, cat/hat, mouse/house, cheese/sneeze, go/hello (every pass), well/dell.
caption: bright high-pitched cheerful young female children's TV singer, clear upfront lead vocals, very clear diction, little vibrato, small happy kids choir on the last chorus, quiet soft backing of ukulele, glockenspiel, soft hand claps and light bass, bouncy action song, simple repetitive sing-along melody, short instrumental breaks between verses, major key, happy, sunny, playful, vocals loud and clear on top of the music
