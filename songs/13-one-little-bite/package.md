## Description
Ollie the octopus isn't so sure about new food! A crunchy carrot, little peas, a broccoli tree and a yellow banana land on his plate. Will he try it? Just one little bite... Mmm! His best friend Bibi the bunny cheers him on, and at the end Ollie holds a fork in every one of his eight arms for a big rainbow plate.

Sing and move along:
- Look at the food and say its name: carrot, peas, broccoli, banana
- Not so sure? Hmm, hmm, hmm!
- Take one little bite and rub your tummy: Mmm!
- Cheer: I tried it and I like it!
