# 46: Little Boy Blue (traditional, public domain: first printed in Tommy Thumb's Pretty Song Book, London, c. 1744; text as in The Real Mother Goose, Rand McNally, 1916 — gentle new lines and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) as Little Boy Blue in a blue cap with a little horn, asleep in a haystack + Bibi (wakes him) + a cow and a sheep, a pig and a duck (drawn in this song's scenes.js). Slot each pass: who wakes him: the cow (moo) / the sheep (baa) / the pig (oink) / the duck (quack).
# Story (visual only): sunny farm, Ollie in a blue cap curls up in the hay with the horn on his belly and snores (z z z) -> the cow nudges his cap off, the sheep nibbles the corn (a corn field and meadow gate) -> the pig snuffles his feet -> the duck quacks in his ear -> Bibi tickles his nose, he wakes with a big stretch, picks up the horn and toots it; all the animals come to the farmyard.
# Teaching: farm animals and their sounds, waking up kindly. Actions: snore, stretch, blow a pretend horn (toot toot).
# Rhymes: horn/corn, Blue/true (every pass); cow/now, sheep/asleep, pig/jig, quack/back (one per pass); finale horn/corn, now/cow.
