# 49: Jingle Bells (traditional, public domain: written by James Lord Pierpont as "The One Horse Open Sleigh", published by Oliver Ditson & Co., Boston, 16 September 1857; the verse lines used are Pierpont's — new verse lines and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) driving a little red sleigh pulled by a friendly white horse (drawn in this song's scenes.js) + Bibi in a scarf. Slot each pass: who waves hello from the snow: Bibi / the crab / the fish / the star.
# Story (visual only): a snowy hill at dusk, Ollie hitches the sleigh -> they dash through the snow, bells jingling (a bell swings under the horse's chin) -> Bibi waves from a snowbank and hops aboard -> the crab, then the fish (in a little fishbowl hat), then the star join -> the full sleigh glides past snowy trees; snowflakes fall; everyone waves.
# Teaching: the jingle bell sound, winter (snow, sleigh), friends joining in. Actions: shake pretend bells, wave hello, bounce on the sleigh.
# Rhymes: way/sleigh (every pass), hello/snow, hey/day; finale snow/go.
caption: bright high-pitched cheerful young female children's TV singer, clear upfront lead vocals, very clear diction, little vibrato, small happy kids choir on the last chorus, quiet soft backing of sleigh bells, ukulele, glockenspiel, soft hand claps and light bass, bouncy action song, simple repetitive sing-along melody, short instrumental breaks between verses, major key, happy, sunny, playful, vocals loud and clear on top of the music
