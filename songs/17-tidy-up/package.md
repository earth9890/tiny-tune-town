## Description
Uh-oh, what a mess! Ollie the octopus and his best friend Bibi the bunny tidy up the playroom: the blocks hop into the toy box, the books slide onto the shelf, the cars zoom into the garage and teddy goes to bed. Then Ollie uses all eight arms to put away eight toys at once. High five!

Sing and move along:
- Pick it up and put it away!
- Plop, plop, plop: blocks in the box
- Slide, slide, slide and zoom, zoom, zoom
- Hush, hush, hush: teddy goes to bed
- Give a big high five when every toy is home!
