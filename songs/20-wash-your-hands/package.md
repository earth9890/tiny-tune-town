## Description
Tap on, pump the soap, and wash! Ollie the octopus scrubs his palms, the backs of his hands, his fingers and his thumbs, and Bibi the bunny joins in. Then it is time for all eight hands at once, a big rinse under the tap, a shake and a pat dry on a soft towel.

Sing and move along:
- Rub, rub, rub your palms and the backs of your hands
- Wiggle your fingers in the soapy bubbles
- Shake, shake, shake your hands dry, then pat pat pat
- Count with Ollie: clean hands, one, two, three!
