# 27: Rain, Rain, Go Away (traditional, public domain (The Real Mother Goose, ill. Blanche Fisher Wright, Rand McNally, 1916; Project Gutenberg #10607) — new verses and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero, at the window) + his family of octopuses (Daddy blue, Mummy purple with a yellow bow, Baby tiny and yellow) + Bibi (friend, joins in pass 3). Slot each pass: who wants to play: Daddy / Mummy / Bibi / Baby, then the rainbow chorus for everyone.
# Story (visual only): a grey rainy day, Ollie sighs at the window -> Daddy builds a blanket fort -> Mummy dances to the raindrops -> Bibi drums on pots and pans -> Baby plays peek-a-boo -> everyone gathers at the window, the rain slows and the sun peeks out -> rainbow in the window -> boots on, everybody runs out and jumps in the puddles (payoff: splash, splash, splash).
# Teaching: family words (Daddy, Mummy, Baby), weather (rain, sun, rainbow), rainbow colours. Actions: wiggle fingers like raindrops, clap, stomp, splash.
# Rhymes: away/day, play/away (the original's own "away / day / play"); dash/splash.
