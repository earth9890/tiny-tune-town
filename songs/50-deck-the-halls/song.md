# 50: Deck the Halls (traditional Welsh tune "Nos Galan", public domain; English words by Thomas Oliphant, first published 1862 in John Thomas's "Welsh Melodies" vol. 2, per Wikipedia "Deck the Halls" — verse 1 as the classic, all other verses and the music new by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) in a cosy winter room + Bibi (hangs the stars, joins pass 2) + fish, crab and turtle (cameos at the window). Slot each pass: what we hang or wrap: holly / stars / bow / bells / the tree.
# Story (visual only): bare room and a big tree -> Ollie hangs holly -> Bibi pops up with stars on a string -> a present gets a red bow -> bells ring on the door -> all eight arms put the star on top -> the tree lights up and everyone cheers.
# Teaching: Christmas decorating words + a happy "fa la la" sing-along. Actions: hang, tie, ring the bells, clap on "fa la".
# Rhymes: holly/jolly, row/glow, bright/tight, ding/sing, tree/me, all/small; refrain la/ha.
caption: bright high-pitched cheerful young female children's TV singer, clear upfront lead vocals, very clear diction, little vibrato, small happy kids choir on the last chorus, quiet soft backing of sleigh bells, glockenspiel, ukulele, soft hand claps and light bass, bouncy action song, simple repetitive sing-along melody, short instrumental breaks between verses, major key, happy, sunny, playful, vocals loud and clear on top of the music
