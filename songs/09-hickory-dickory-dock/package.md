## Description
Tick tock! Ollie the octopus has a big, friendly grandfather clock in his living room. Every hour a new friend runs up the clock: a mouse, a bird, a frog and a duck. Count the bongs, make the animal sounds, and watch every friend climb onto Ollie's eight arms. Then everybody dances by the clock!

Sing and move along:
- Swing your arm like a pendulum: tick tock, tick tock!
- Run your fingers up and down like the mouse
- Count the hours: one, two, three, four
- Squeak, tweet, ribbit and quack with the animals
- Dance round the clock with your friends!
