## Description
Ollie the octopus opens a storybook, and out hops a fluffy little lamb! Everywhere that Ollie goes, the lamb is sure to go: down the slide at the park, into the shopping cart at the shop, and all the way to school. Sing the classic rhyme, say "baa baa baa!" with the lamb, and give your best friend a great big hug.

Sing and move along:
- Say "baa, baa, baa!" with the little lamb
- Skip along to the park, the shop and school
- Count the eight things Ollie holds in his eight arms at the shop
- Give a big hug: hug, hug, hug!
