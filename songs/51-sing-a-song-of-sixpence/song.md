# Cast: Ollie (hero) at a royal tea party + the king (counting coins), the queen (bread and honey), the maid with the washing line, four-and-twenty friendly blackbirds, all drawn in this song's scenes.js; Bibi joins at the finale. Slot each pass: who is in the story: the birds in the pie / the king and queen / the maid / Ollie.
# Story (visual only): a big pie on the king's table -> the crust lifts and the blackbirds pop out singing -> the king counts coins while the queen eats bread and honey, and the birds sit on their chairs -> the maid hangs washing in the garden and a blackbird hops by, she laughs -> Ollie waves all 8 arms and sings with the birds -> finale: everyone claps, the birds fly up high into a rainbow, wave bye-bye.
# Teaching: counting (a king counts coins, birds count 1-2-3), bird sounds (tweet). Actions: tweet like a bird, flap your wings, clap, wave bye-bye.
