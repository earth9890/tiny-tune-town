# 29: Are You Sleeping? (idea from the traditional round "Frère Jacques / Are You Sleeping": the TUNE is public domain (French manuscript c. 1780, printed 1811, La Clé du Caveau); the French words are public domain (1825 Aimé Paris; 1860 Lebouc, Récréations de l'enfance). The English "Brother John" wording could not be dated before 1929, so ALL lyrics here are new, original words by Tiny Tune Town and the melody is our own.)
# Cast: Ollie (hero, the first sleepyhead) + Bibi (sleeping in her bunny bed) + a fish in the bowl + the sun behind a hill. Slot each pass: who is still in bed (Ollie, Bibi, the fish, the sun).
# Story (visual only): early morning, the church-style bell on the hill swings. Ollie hides under his quilt (eight arms stick out), Bibi hugs a pillow, the fish snoozes in a tiny bed, the sun is a half-circle under a blanket of cloud. Each pass the bell's ding-dang-dong wakes the next friend, who yawns, stretches and joins Ollie at the window. Last pass: everyone awake, sun up, big stretch, breakfast smell. Teaching: morning routine, ding dang dong sounds. Actions: yawn, stretch, ring the bell, clap.
# Rhymes: bed/head, dong/along, everyone/sun, hooray/day.
