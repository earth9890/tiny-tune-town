## Description
Oh no, the bridge is broken down! Ollie the octopus and his friends come to help. They build it up with sticks, then bricks, then shells, then sand, stacking high row by row, until everybody can walk over the bridge again. Hooray!

Sing and move along:
- Stack your hands high, row by row
- Pat, pat, pat the sand
- March over the bridge on the spot
- Clap, clap, clap when the bridge is up
- Dance with the friends
