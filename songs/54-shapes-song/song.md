# 54: Shapes in the Sea (original song by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) swimming round a bright reef + Bibi (friend, in a snorkel mask, points at things) + fish and crab (cameos carrying shapes), drawn in this song's scenes.js. Slot each pass: the shape and what it is: circle (a pearl) / square (a treasure box) / triangle (a sail) / star (a starfish) / heart (a heart-shaped shell).
# Story (visual only): Ollie spots a glowing shape drifting past -> a round pearl rolls to him -> a square box with a key is carried by the crab -> a triangle sail goes by on a tiny boat -> a starfish waves five points -> a heart-shaped shell floats up and Ollie hugs it -> all five shapes line up and bob together as friends.
# Teaching: five shapes (circle, square, triangle, star, heart) + counting sides (4, 3, 5) + count 1-2-3. Actions: draw the shape in the air with one finger, then hug the heart.
# Rhymes: see/sea, me/three, me/sea (every pass); finale star/far, you/blue.
