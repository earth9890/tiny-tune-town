# 08: Itsy Bitsy Spider (traditional, public domain (first printed 1910 as "Spider Song" in A. W. North, "Camp and Camino in Lower California"; the water-spout wording reported from 1912) — new verses and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) + Bibi (friend) in a garden by a little house with a water spout; Itsy, a round friendly spider. Slot each pass: the weather: rain / wind / snow / sun, then rain + sun = rainbow.
# Story (visual only): Itsy tries to climb the spout -> rain washes her down (Ollie holds an umbrella) -> wind blows her off (Bibi's scarf flaps) -> snow slides her down (scarves, snowman) -> sunshine, sunglasses, she nearly makes it -> rainbow, she climbs to the very top and waves.
# Teaching: weather words (rain, wind, snow, sun, rainbow). Actions: fingers climb up, hands rain down, arms whoosh, shiver brrr, arms make the sun and the rainbow.
caption: cheerful children's nursery sing-along, warm clear young female kids-show lead singer, very clear diction, little vibrato, small happy kids choir on the last chorus, marimba, ukulele, soft hand claps, light acoustic bass, glockenspiel, simple repetitive sing-along melody, short instrumental breaks between verses, major key, happy, sunny, playful, clean bright mix
