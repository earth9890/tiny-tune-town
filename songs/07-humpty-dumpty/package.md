## Description
Humpty Dumpty wobbles on the garden wall and... oops! He tumbles softly onto the grass. Ollie the octopus and Bibi the bunny run to help with a bandage, a great big hug and a lift back up, because that's what friends do!

Sing and move along:
- Sit on a wall and wobble side to side
- Oops! A soft tumble
- Hug, hug, hug, and lift him up
- Cheer: all of his friends put Humpty together again!
