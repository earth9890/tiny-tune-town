# 43: The ABC Song (public domain: the tune is the old French air "Ah! vous dirai-je, Maman" (Les Amusements d'une heure et demy, Paris, 1761); the alphabet song was first copyrighted as "The A.B.C., a German air with variations for the flute", Charles Bradlee, Boston, 1835. The 1835 print is sheet music and I could not confirm any printed words from it, so here only the alphabet itself and the "sing along with me" couplet idea are traditional; every picture line and the closing couplet are new, and the melody is new by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) at a seaside chalk board + Bibi. Slot each pass: the two picture letters (A apple, B ball / C cat, D dog / E egg, F fish).
# Story (visual only): Ollie draws big letters on a chalk board -> A and B pop to life: an apple rolls, a ball bounces -> Bibi draws C and D: a cat and a dog -> E and F: an egg sits in a nest, a fish swims through the bubbles -> a finger points to each letter as it is sung; last pass: all the pictures wave from the board.
# Teaching: the alphabet in order, first sounds of apple, ball, cat, dog, egg, fish. Actions: point to each letter, wave to each picture.
# Rhymes: G/P and V/Z (letter names that rhyme, "-ee"), three/ABC (every pass), round/ground, sweet/feet, nest/best.
