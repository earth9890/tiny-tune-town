## Description
Ollie the octopus puts on his chef's hat and bakes a cake for every friend! Pat it, mark it with a letter and pop it in the oven: O for Ollie, B for Bibi, C for Crab and T for Turtle. Then hot cross buns come out of the oven and all the cakes go on the table. Happy baking!

Sing and move along:
- Clap on the beat: pat-a-cake, pat-a-cake!
- Draw the letter in the air: O, B, C, T
- Pop it in the oven, then clap, clap, clap!
- Count the hot cross buns: one, two!
