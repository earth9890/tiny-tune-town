## Description
It is a rainy day, but there is lots to do indoors! Daddy builds a blanket fort, Mummy dances, Bibi drums on pots and Baby plays peek-a-boo. Then the rain goes away, a rainbow appears and everybody pulls on their boots to splash in the puddles.

Sing and move along:
- Wave your hands: rain, rain, go away
- Build a pretend blanket fort
- Dance, dance, dance
- Drum on pots and pans
- Peek-a-boo: look, the sun!
- Jump in puddles: splash, splash, splash!
