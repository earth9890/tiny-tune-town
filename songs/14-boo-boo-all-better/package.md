## Description
Bibi the bunny slips on the slide and bumps her knee. Boo-hoo! Ollie the octopus is there to help: wash it, kiss it, hug it, and a star bandage makes it all better. Then Crab bumps an elbow, Ollie bumps his finger (and Bibi helps him!), and Turtle bumps a nose. A gentle, caring song for little bumps and big feelings.

Sing and move along:
- Point to your knee, your elbow, your finger and your nose
- There, there, there: a gentle pat for a friend
- Wash it, kiss it, hug it: all better!
- Give a big hug and a big smile
