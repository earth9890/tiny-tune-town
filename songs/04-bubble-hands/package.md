## Description
Wash your hands with Bibi the bunny! Turn on the water, add some soap and scrub along with the song. Count to ten while the bubbles grow, then rinse and dry for squeaky clean hands.

Sing and do it along with Bibi:
- splish splash your hands under the water;
- squish the soap into big bubbles;
- rub your palms round and round;
- rub the backs of your hands;
- wiggle your fingers in between;
- twist your thumbs;
- scrub and count from one to ten;
- whoosh the bubbles down the sink;
- pat pat pat your hands dry;
- clap along and shout "Yay, clean hands!"
