## Description
Itsy the little spider wants to climb the water spout, but the rain washes her down, the wind blows her onto Ollie's head and the snow slides her into a snowdrift! Ollie the octopus and his best friend Bibi the bunny cheer her on with umbrellas, scarves and sunglasses, and when a rainbow comes out, Itsy climbs all the way to the very top.

Sing and move along:
- Climb your fingers up, up, up the water spout
- Wiggle your fingers down like the rain
- Whoosh your arms like the wind and shiver, brrr!
- Make a big round sun with your arms
- Learn the weather: rain, wind, snow, sunshine and a rainbow
