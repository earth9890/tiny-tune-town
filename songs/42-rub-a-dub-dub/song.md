# 42: Rub a Dub Dub (traditional, public domain: first printed 1798 as "Dub a dub dub" in James Hook's The Christmas Box, vol. 2, London; a later text is in Mother Goose Melodies, Boston, 1833 — the three men become three friends, new verses and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) + Bibi + Crab, three friends squeezed in one big round tub of bubbles, drawn in this song's scenes.js. Slot each pass: the body part to scrub: toes / knees / thumbs / hair.
# Story (visual only): Ollie fills a big tub and hops in, Bibi and Crab climb in too (a splash) -> bubbles pile up on toes -> a rubber duck floats by, Crab scrubs knees -> bubble thumbs -> bubble hair hats; the tub is full of foam and everyone pops out clean with towels.
# Teaching: body parts, the bath routine, counting three. Actions: rub, scrub, splash, wash hair.
# Rhymes: dub/tub, be/three, toes/nose, knees/please, thumb/come, hair/everywhere, hooray/day (every pass).
