## Description
Time to get dressed! Ollie the octopus puts on his pants, pulls his shirt over his head (peek-a-boo!), wiggles his toes in his socks, snaps his shoes, zips up his coat and pats his nice red hat. With eight arms, he is dressed in no time and ready to go out!

Sing and move along:
- Pull your pants up, one, two, three
- Peek-a-boo through your shirt
- Wiggle your toes
- Tap, tap, tap your shoes
- Zip up your coat with a big hug
- Pat, pat, pat your hat
