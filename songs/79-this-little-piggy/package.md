## Description
This little piggy went to market! After bath time Daddy plays the toe game with Nomi on the sofa: one little piggy for each toe, all the way home, and a tickle on the nose. Then Nomi counts Daddy's big toes and big brother Kofi's toes, and the whole family wiggles and counts to five. A cosy, giggly sing-along of the classic nursery rhyme for babies, toddlers and preschoolers.

Sing and move along:
- Touch one toe for each little piggy
- Run your fingers up for wee, wee, wee
- Wiggle your toes
- Count to five: one, two, three, four, five
- Tickle, tickle on your nose!
