# 19: Hey Diddle Diddle (traditional, public domain (Mother Goose's Melody, London, c. 1765; text as in The Real Mother Goose, Rand McNally, 1916, and Halliwell's Nursery Rhymes of England, 1842) — new verses and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) on a moonlit hill + the classic's cat (fiddle), cow, little dog, dish and spoon, drawn in this song's scenes.js; Bibi joins the finale. Slot each pass: who jumps over the moon next: cow / cat / dog / Ollie, each with its sound (moo / meow / woof / flap).
# Story (visual only): Ollie watches the big smiling moon over the hill -> the cow jumps over it -> the cat (fiddle and all) jumps with the cow -> the dog jumps too -> Ollie flaps all 8 arms and jumps over the moon -> finale: everyone, Bibi too, jumps together; the dish and spoon wave bye-bye.
# Teaching: animal sounds (moo, meow, woof) as call and response + jumping; count 1-2-3 in the finale. Actions: jump, play a pretend fiddle, say the sounds, flap your arms.
# Rhymes: you-pattern too/do (every pass), fiddle/diddle (every pass), moon/spoon (the classic, every pass); finale three/me, cow/now, diddle/middle, moon/spoon.
