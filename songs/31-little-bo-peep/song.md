# 31: Little Bo-Peep (traditional, public domain (first printed c. 1805; text as in The Real Mother Goose, Rand McNally, 1916, and Halliwell's Nursery Rhymes of England, 1842) — new verses and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) helps Bo-Peep (a little shepherd girl with a crook, drawn in this song's scenes.js) on a green hill + Bibi. Slot each pass: where we look and how many sheep we find: tree 1 / hill 2 / gate 3 / hay 4 / sun 5.
# Story (visual only): Bo-Peep sits by an empty meadow, worried -> Ollie points with all 8 arms: behind a tree, one sheep -> up on the hill, two -> by the gate, three -> in the hay, four -> in the sun, five -> all five sheep come trotting home wagging their tails, Bo-Peep hugs every one.
# Teaching: counting 1-5, places (behind, up, by, in). Actions: hand over eyes to look, point, say "baa!", wag a tail.
# Rhymes: sheep/weep (every pass), tree/see, hill/still, gate/wait, hay/play, sun/one, alone/home (the classic's own near rhyme), small/all.
