# 44: Mary, Mary, How Does Your Garden Grow? (traditional, public domain: "Mary, Mary, quite contrary" first printed in Tommy Thumb's Pretty Song Book, London, c. 1744; text as in The Real Mother Goose, Rand McNally, 1916 — "contrary", the shells and the maids are left out; new verses and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) visits Mary, a little gardener in a straw hat and wellies (a new character drawn in this song's scenes.js, never reused for another role) + Bibi helps with the watering can. Slot each pass: the flower colour: red tulips / yellow daisies / blue bells / pink roses.
# Story (visual only): Ollie waves over a garden fence -> Mary and Ollie plant seeds -> a rain cloud and a sunbeam, a green shoot -> red tulips pop up in a row, Bibi waters them -> yellow daisies, then blue bells, then pink roses, a snail and a ladybird visit -> a whole rainbow garden, everyone sits in the middle.
# Teaching: colours, plants need water and sun, counting rows. Actions: dig, water, stretch up like a flower.
# Rhymes: grow/row, sun/everyone (every pass); finale sun/everyone, rain/again.
