# 09: Hickory Dickory Dock (traditional, public domain (1744, Tommy Thumb's Pretty Song Book) — new verses and music by Tiny Tune Town)
# Cast: Ollie (hero) in a cosy living room by a big smiling grandfather clock + the mouse, bird, frog and duck. Slot each pass: the hour + the animal + its sound (one/mouse/squeak, two/bird/tweet, three/frog/ribbit, four/duck/quack).
# Story (visual only): Ollie hears the clock tick and peeks at it -> each pass a new friend climbs the clock, it strikes the hour (big number), the friend slides down into Ollie's arms -> the friends line up on the rug -> finale: everyone dances by the clock and the clock's face beams.
# Teaching: count the hours 1-4 + animal sounds. Actions: tick tock with your arms, run up and down with your fingers, make the animal sound, dance.
caption: bouncy action song for kids, warm clear young female kids-show lead singer, very clear diction, little vibrato, small happy kids choir on the last chorus, ukulele, glockenspiel, woodblock tick tock, soft hand claps, light bass, simple repetitive sing-along melody, short instrumental breaks between verses, major key, happy, sunny, playful, clean bright mix
