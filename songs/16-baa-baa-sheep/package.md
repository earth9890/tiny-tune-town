## Description
Ollie the octopus and Bibi the bunny visit a sunny farm meadow, where a black sheep, a white sheep, a pink sheep and a rainbow sheep each give three bags of wool. Count the bags, learn the colours, then watch Ollie knit a rainbow scarf with all eight arms!

Sing and move along:
- Say "Baa, baa!" with the sheep
- Count the bags on your fingers: one, two, three!
- Name each sheep's colour: black, white, pink and rainbow
- Wave and say "Thank you, little sheep!"
