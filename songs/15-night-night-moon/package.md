## Description
It's bedtime in Ollie's cosy room! One by one his friends yawn and fall asleep: Bibi the bunny, the little fish, the twinkly star and the big moon. Then it's Ollie's turn to close his eyes. A slow, gentle lullaby to help little ones wind down for sleep.

Sing and move along:
- Give a big, slow yawn: yawn, yawn, yawn
- Finger to your lips: hush, hush, hush
- Close your eyes and snuggle down
- Wave night-night to everyone you love
