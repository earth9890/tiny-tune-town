## Description
Ollie the octopus has eight arms, so he can brush with eight toothbrushes! Brush the top teeth, the bottom teeth, the front and the back with Ollie and his best friend Bibi the bunny, then rinse, spit and show your big bright smile.

Sing and move along:
- Brush up and down: scrub, scrub, scrub!
- Swish, swish, swish, round and round
- Top, bottom, front and back
- Rinse, spit and give your biggest smile!
