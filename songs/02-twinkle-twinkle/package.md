## Description
Sing, twinkle and count the stars with Pip the sleepy bunny and Twinkle, the friendliest star in the sky! A gentle bedtime version of Twinkle Twinkle Little Star for babies, toddlers and preschoolers.

Sing along and do the actions:
- Twinkle your fingers up high in every chorus
- Wave bye-bye to the sun
- Count the stars from 1 to 3 and clap, clap
- Play peek-a-boo with your hands
- Yawn, stretch and say goodnight to the star
