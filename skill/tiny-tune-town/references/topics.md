# Topics: what to make

## Where the views are (research/cocomelon.md §1, §9)
- Of the biggest channel's top 20:
  - **11 are daily-routine or life-moment songs**: bath 7.7 B, vegetables 3.5 B, playground, peek-a-boo, sick, bedtime, swimming.
  - **9 are public-domain classics**: Baa Baa 5.0 B, Old MacDonald, Twinkle, Bingo, Humpty…
- **Parents search for routine songs** to win daily battles: bath, teeth, food, sleep, potty, tidy-up, boo-boos. These are tools, not just entertainment.
- Classics win search because parents type the rhyme's name.
- Versions of a hit (a new theme or setting) reuse proven demand.

## Rights check (always)
- **Public domain:** verify the text was published **before 1929** and note the year and source in `song.md`. Write our own melody and new verses anyway; the traditional melody is fine only if it is itself public domain.
- **Never:**
  - Baby Shark (in copyright).
  - Wheels on the Bus until checked (Verna Hills, 1939).
  - Happy Birthday: public domain since 2016, but write our own birthday song instead.
  - Anything a channel wrote itself: Bath Song, "Yes Yes" songs, Sick Song.
  - Licensed songs (Bananaphone).
  - Their character names and their "doo doo doo" hook style.
- **Originals:** our own words, our own titles.
